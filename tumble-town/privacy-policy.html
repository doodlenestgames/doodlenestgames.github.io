Privacy Policy for Tumble Town

Effective date: 21 October 2026
App: Tumble Town (com.doodlenestgames.tumbletown)
Developer: Doodle Nest Games
Contact: doodlenestgames@gmail.com

Tumble Town is a simple play app for young children (aged 5 and under), with five play zones: Match Me!, Shape & Color, Count With Me, Drag & Drop, and Doodle Studio. This policy explains, in plain words, what the app does with information. The short version: Tumble Town collects no personal data, shows no advertising, and sends nothing anywhere.

We do not collect personal data

The app does not collect, store on any server, sell, or share any personal data. In particular, the app never asks for or records:

  • names, ages, dates of birth, or free-text entries of any kind;
  • email addresses, accounts, usernames, passwords, or sign-in details of any kind — the app has no accounts and no sign-in;
  • device identifiers, advertising IDs, install IDs, or any other identifiers;
  • location, contacts, photos, videos, microphone or camera input;
  • timestamps, play counts, session durations, scores, or usage analytics of any kind.

The app works completely offline

The release version of Tumble Town released on Google Play does not include the Android INTERNET permission (it is deliberately removed, along with the advertising ID permission and network-state permission). The app therefore cannot connect to the internet or transmit data to us or to anyone else.

What the app saves on the device only

So that a child's play is not lost when the app is closed, the app saves two small files in its own private storage on the device itself:

  • Play progress: which of the app's 24 stickers the child has earned, and which level each play zone should reopen at. This file contains only sticker IDs from a fixed list and four small numbers — no names, ages, identifiers, dates, or counters.
  • The child's drawing: the strokes and stamps of the current Doodle Studio canvas, so the drawing is still there next time. The app has no share, export, or send feature for drawings.

These files never leave the device through the app. We cannot see them, and no third party receives them from the app.

Device backups (controlled by you, not by us)

If you have turned on Android's own device backup (for example, Google backup) in your device settings, Android itself may include the app's local files in the backup stored in your own Google Account, so they can be restored if you move to a new device. That backup is a feature of your device and your Google Account, controlled by your settings. Doodle Nest Games does not receive or have access to it.

Advertising, analytics, and third parties

Tumble Town contains no advertising, no analytics or crash-reporting services, no social features, no chat, no external links, and no in-app purchases. The app uses only local software libraries needed to play sounds and save the files described above on the device; those libraries do not collect or transmit data.

Children's privacy

Tumble Town is designed for children aged 5 and under and is committed to following the Google Play Families policy. Because the app collects no personal data from anyone, including children, there is no child's personal data for us to use, disclose, or delete.

Deleting the app's local files

You can delete everything the app has saved at any time by clearing the app's storage in Android Settings > Apps > Tumble Town > Storage > Clear storage, or by uninstalling the app, which removes its files from the device.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Tumble Town changes any of the practices described here, we will update this page first, with a new effective date, and update the app's disclosures on Google Play to match.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or the app can be sent to doodlenestgames@gmail.com.
