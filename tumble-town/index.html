Five play zones

Every zone has six levels — and every level is open from the very start. Nothing is locked, nothing is timed.

Match Me!

A first pairs game with shapes and colours. Find the two that go together.

Shape & Color

Look at the picture and tap the shape — or the colour — that matches.

Count With Me

Counting with friendly pictures, with a gentle nudge if a little finger slips.

Drag & Drop

Sorting and moving pieces into place, one satisfying drop at a time.

Doodle Studio

A free drawing canvas with chunky stamps — plus a sticker book to fill.

Gentle by design

No way to fail

There are no scores, no timers and no losing. A soft hint appears if a child gets stuck, and every level can be finished at their own pace.

Everything open

All six levels in every zone are open from the first install — no locked doors, no waiting, no paying to unlock.

24 stickers to earn

Finishing levels earns stickers for the sticker book — a small celebration for small achievements.

For parents

Tumble Town was built to be the calm corner of the app store.

  • No ads and no in-app purchases — nothing to tap by accident, nothing to buy.
  • No accounts or sign-in — the game never asks who your child is.
  • Works completely offline — the app has no internet access at all.
  • Collects no personal data — play progress and drawings are saved only on your device, and are never uploaded anywhere.
  • Made for ages 5 and under — big friendly buttons, simple words, patient pacing.
